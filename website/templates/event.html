Halcyon Drift

Rock / Punk · Saturday 12 September 2026 · The Zoo, Fortitude Valley

About this gig

Halcyon Drift have spent two years playing to eighty people in rooms with bad ceilings, and this is the last show before they leave for the east coast run. The new material is louder and considerably less polite than the EP.

Paper Ferns open with a short set of their reverb-drenched instrumentals, and Slow Tourist play second. Doors at 7:30pm, first act on at 8:00pm sharp — the venue has a hard curfew and the headline set will not be pushed back.
