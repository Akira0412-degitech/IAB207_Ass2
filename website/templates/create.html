Common mistakes to avoid

  • Tokenistic or generic wording that could apply to any location
  • Misidentifying the Traditional Custodians of this specific venue
  • Confusing an Acknowledgement of Country with a Welcome to Country (a Welcome can only be given by Traditional Owners themselves)
  • Not accounting for situations where multiple groups claim custodianship of the same area
